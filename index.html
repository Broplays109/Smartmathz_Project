Rice

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of long-grain white rice
  • 2 cups of water
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil (optional)

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice thoroughly under cold water.
  2. Add two cups of water for every cup of rice.
  3. Bring the water to a boil in a pot.
  4. Stir in the rice and add a pinch of salt.
  5. Cover with a lid and reduce heat to low.
  6. Simmer for 15 minutes without lifting the lid.
  7. Remove from heat and let sit for 5 minutes.
  8. Fluff with a fork and serve hot.

Pizza

Ingredients

  • 1 pre-made pizza dough ball
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough onto a floured surface.
  3. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the crust.
  4. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  5. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden brown.
  6. Top with fresh basil and a drizzle of olive oil before slicing.

Eba

Ingredients

  • Garri (cassava flakes)
  • Hot boiling water

Steps

  1. Boil the water in a pot or kettle.
  2. Pour the hot water into a clean bowl.
  3. Sprinkle the garri smoothly into the water.
  4. Let it sit for 1 minute to absorb water.
  5. Stir firmly with a wooden spatula until smooth.

Pando

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of Poundo Yam flour
  • 2 cups of water (adjust for desired thickness)

Steps

  1. Boil water: Pour the water into a small pot and bring it to a rolling boil.
  2. Reduce heat: Turn the stove heat down to low to prevent instant burning.
  3. Add flour: Pour the poundo flour into the boiling water while stirring continuously.
  4. Mix well: Use a wooden spatula to stir vigorously until a thick, smooth dough forms.
  5. Steam: Add a tablespoon of hot water around the edges, cover the pot, and let it steam on low heat for 2 minutes.
  6. Final mix: Stir thoroughly one last time until the dough becomes stretchy and smooth.
  7. Serve: Scoop into plastic wrap or a bowl, mold it into a ball, and serve hot with soup.

Classic Apple Pie Recipe

Ingredients

  • Pie crusts: 2 refrigerated or homemade pastry crusts.
  • Apples: 6 cups of thinly sliced, peeled baking apples (like Granny Smith).
  • Granulated sugar: 3/4 cup to sweeten the fruit filling.
  • All-purpose flour: 2 tablespoons to thicken the pie juices.
  • Ground cinnamon: 1 teaspoon for warm spice flavor.
  • Salt: 1/4 teaspoon to balance the sweetness.
  • Lemon juice: 1 tablespoon to keep the apples bright and fresh.
  • Unsalted butter: 1 tablespoon, cut into small pieces to dot the filling.

Steps

  1. Preheat and prep: Heat your oven to 425°F (220°C) and fit one pastry crust into a 9-inch pie plate.
  2. Mix the filling: Toss the sliced apples, sugar, flour, cinnamon, salt, and lemon juice together in a large bowl.
  3. Fill the crust: Pour the apple mixture into the pie plate and dot the top with the small butter pieces.
  4. Top the pie: Place the second crust over the filling, trim the edges, and crimp them together to seal.
  5. Vent the top: Cut a few small slits in the top crust with a knife to let steam escape during baking.
  6. Bake: Bake for 40 to 45 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the filling bubbles through the slits.
  7. Cool: Let the pie cool completely on a wire rack for at least 2 hours before slicing so the filling sets.

Classic Vanilla Cake Recipe

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour: 2 and 1/2 cups for the cake structure.
  • Baking powder: 2 and 1/2 teaspoons to help the layers rise.
  • Salt: 1/2 teaspoon to balance the sweetness.
  • Unsalted butter: 1 cup, softened at room temperature.
  • Granulated sugar: 1 and 1/2 cups for sweetness and moisture.
  • Eggs: 4 large eggs to provide structure and richness.
  • Vanilla extract: 1 tablespoon for the primary flavor.
  • Whole milk: 1 cup to create a smooth, tender batter.

Steps

  • Preheat and prep: Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C), grease two 9-inch round cake pans, and line the bottoms with parchment paper.
  • Combine dry ingredients: Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt together in a medium bowl, then set aside.
  • Cream butter and sugar: Beat the softened butter and granulated sugar together in a large bowl until light and fluffy.
  • Add eggs and vanilla: Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition, then stir in the vanilla extract.
  • Alternate wet and dry: Gradually add the dry ingredients and milk to the butter mixture, alternating between them and mixing just until combined.
  • Bake the layers: Divide the batter evenly between your prepared pans and bake for 25 to 30 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  • Cool completely: Let the cakes cool in their pans for 10 minutes, then turn them out onto a wire rack to cool fully before frosting.
